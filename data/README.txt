Place the ORIGINAL EBA files of each edition in its own folder:

data/year_2019/   tr_oth*.csv  tr_cre*.csv  tr_mrk*.csv  tr_sov*.csv  SDD*.xlsx  TR_Metadata*.xlsx
data/year_2020/   (same files; names may carry suffixes such as tr_oth_2.csv)
data/year_2021/   ...

The data files may be .csv (as downloaded) or .xlsx (Excel version of the same file). Do not rename columns, do not convert encodings.
